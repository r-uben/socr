# #1383: scan completeness check, known limit and the layer-box diagnosis

## Known limit (accepted in the #1381 ruling)
`hidden_layer_ink_accounted` fails on ink OUTSIDE the invisible layer's character boxes. Ink
under a box is never checked: raster `(7)` with layer `7` and reading `7` passes, as do `-7`,
`1.5` read as `15`, small print inside a large box and a superscript inside a line box. A lone
mark under the residual size floor outside every box is not seen. All of these need the old OCR
and the fresh reading to make the same mistake. Stated in `verdict.prior_ocr_layer_agreement`.

## Why the layer's boxes miss real glyphs (measured, no behaviour change)
Pages p071 and p001 of the 100-page replay; the p071 text equals its layer token for token and the
check still fails on 3 dark components. They are real glyph parts, not scan specks.

| residual | ink (pt) | nearest layer char box (pt) |
|---|---|---|
| comma after "steel prices" | x 427.7-429.6, y 90.2-93.1 | ',' x 425.2-428.1, y 79.1-95.1; next char is a space (not painted) |
| comma | x 383.0-385.4, y 236.2-239.0 | ',' x 380.9-383.9; next char a space |
| last "l" of "pharmaceutical" | x 328.8-331.2, y 258.2-266.9 | 'l' x 326.2-329.5; next char a space |

Each ink lies 1.5-2.6 pt to the RIGHT of its character's box, in the gap the layer fills with a
space, which `visible_char_boxes` drops. Vertically the ink is inside the 16 pt box.

Sensitivity (same page, same residual test): widening every box horizontally by 1 pt per side
takes p071 from 3 residuals to 0 and p001 from 15 to 0; widening vertically by 2 or 4 pt changes
nothing. Painting the layer's own space characters as well also gives 0 and 0 (p018, which was
clean, stays 0).

Causes ruled out: not a render-scale or rounding offset (boxes and ink go through the same matrix,
`_to_pix` floor/ceil adds at most 1 px = 0.5 pt, the offsets are 3-5 px, and only horizontal);
not a box source that drops punctuation (the commas have boxes). The cause is the old OCR's boxes:
advance-width boxes that drift against the scan's real glyph positions, with the overhang falling
in the space the box source drops.

## Candidate fix (not applied)
Paint the layer's own space characters too: no new threshold. It trades completeness: ink that
overlaps a layer space box (a comma or period the OCR dropped right after a word) would count as
accounted. Whether that trade is acceptable is an owner decision.
