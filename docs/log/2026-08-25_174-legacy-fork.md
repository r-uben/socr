# Ruling on the #174 fork — DELETE

Owner's decision, 2026-08-25. The legacy deterministic stack is **deleted**, not gated
behind `--legacy-routing`.

This closes ticket **P1** of section L and unblocks stream R.

## What was decided against

Keeping the legacy path behind a single `--legacy-routing` flag. Rejected because a second
control loop is the mechanism that produces the flag divergence #142 exists to kill: a flag
wired in one branch and not the other is invisible until a user hits it.

## Why the owner ruled delete

The three user-visible features on the legacy stack are the *old* way of buying quality —
run several OCR engines, compare their answers, retry the bad pages. The agentic path buys
quality differently: one page at a time, with a judge model deciding whether to escalate.
Keeping both means maintaining two answers to the same question.
