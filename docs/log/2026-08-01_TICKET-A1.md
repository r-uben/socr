## A second finding, not in the ticket text

`test_wrapped_label_is_scored_the_same_as_unwrapped` (**xfail**, own fixture,
own reason string — does **not** name TICKET-B2, a different root cause):
building "wrap a label across two lines" (the ticket's fifth benign bullet)
surfaces a defect distinct from B2. `native_rows_from_page` never reconstructs
a label genuinely split across two visual bands — whichever band carries the
row's values keeps only the text on that band, and the other line is silently
dropped from the ground truth. A perfect transcription of a wrapped-label row
therefore scores as if the row were missing.

Checked both geometries before concluding this: at the leading used by the
existing regression fixture (`test_a_wrapped_label_is_not_merged_with_the_line_above`,
9pt gap) the bands stay separate and the value-bearing line ("debt") is kept
alone, dropping "Central government net"; tightening the gap far enough to
force the bands to merge (6pt) does not help either — merging sorts the
combined band's words by x, producing "Central debt government net"
(scrambled order), not the correct label. There is no gap at which
`_rows_in_region`'s current two-step (band, then last-non-numeric-word split)
recovers a genuinely two-line label correctly. `270cdab` ("don't merge a
wrapped label with the line above it") stops the *worse* outcome
(interleaving with an *unrelated* neighbouring line) but was never a full fix
for a label that legitimately spans two lines of its own.

Landed as a second xfail rather than silently working around it or silently
dropping the bullet, and flagged here per the brief's "flag anything that
contradicts the ticket text" — not filed as a numbered ticket; that decision
belongs to the plan owner (candidate ticket ID reused nowhere in this plan;
suggest B5 or folding into B3/B4 since it is also a row-identity problem, not
an escalation-decision problem, so C1 is the wrong stream).
